### 2.2 «How we supply chemicals» (5 шагов, тот же стиль, что «ONE PROCESS, FIVE STEPS»)
1. **Specification** — you send the grade, quantity, destination and your tender requirements.
2. **Documents first** — TDS, SDS and a sample COA before any price; free samples on request.
3. **Quotation** — one basis: Incoterm, pack, lead time, document set.
4. **Production and inspection** — batch COA from the plant; third-party inspection (SGS, Intertek or Bureau Veritas) where required.
5. **Delivery** — by sea, rail or truck, FOB or CIF your port or delivered to site, documents with the goods.

### 2.3 «For tenders» (отдельный блок)
